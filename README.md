# 🇸🇴 Introduction to Somalia History

A simple project that introduces **Somalia**, its geography, regions, and major cities.

## 📖 About Somalia

Somalia is a country located in the **Horn of Africa**. It shares borders with:

* 🇪🇹 Ethiopia
* 🇰🇪 Kenya
* 🇩🇯 Djibouti

Somalia has one of the longest coastlines in Africa, stretching along the **Indian Ocean and the Red Sea**.

## 🗺️ Regions of Somalia

Somalia consists of **18 regions**, established by the former central government of Somalia.

Some well-known regions include:

1. **Banaadir** — Home to the capital city, Mogadishu.
2. **Bari** — One of the largest regions in Somalia.
3. **Nugaal** — Home to Garowe, the capital of Puntland.
4. **Mudug** — A region administered between Galmudug and Puntland.

## 🏙️ Major Cities

* **Mogadishu** — Capital of Somalia.
* **Hargeisa** — Major city in northern Somalia.
* **Kismayo** — Major coastal city in southern Somalia.
